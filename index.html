Pace — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Pace is a reading goal app made by an independent developer. The short version: your data stays on your device, and we don't collect anything.

What Pace stores, and where

Everything you create in Pace, including your reading speed, books, goals, reading sessions, and settings, is stored only on your device. There are no accounts, no cloud sync, and no servers of ours that ever see your data. Deleting the app deletes your data.

Book search

When you search for a book, Pace sends your search terms to the Google Books API to look up titles, authors, page counts, and cover images. That request goes to Google and is subject to Google's privacy policy. Pace sends only the search text and the app's identifier, nothing about you, your goals, or your reading.

What we don't do

Pace has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party SDKs, and collects no personal information. We can't see how you use the app, and we like it that way.

Notifications

If you turn on reading reminders, they are scheduled locally on your device. No notification data leaves your phone.
